Black Mirror is presented here as a transcript-grounded research programme rather than as a formal system that begins by postulating finite objects. Closure is the originless unity from which local and global perspectives, transformations, returns, and later measurements become distinguishable. A transcript quotation establishes the intended relation. A plain-language translation states what an implementation or empirical study would have to show. A data table reports the available evidence and the boundary of the claim.

This method is applied to the Potential Gate, ball–hair return, perspectival AGI, semantic tokens, anti-gaming tokenomics, Xenopus-to-Xenobot re-embodiment, coevolution, Chaitin-style string ordering, Topos–Turing mutual admissibility, and the Slearn–Black Mirror–MainStreet conversion loop.

They do not establish that Black Mirror closure is loop quantum gravity or that ball–hair vocabulary describes literal astrophysical white holes. The correspondence remains comparative and OPEN.

# 1. The socioeconomic problem after automation

The economic problem is not merely which tasks machines can perform. It is which relations continue to authorize meaning and value when repetition becomes cheap.

Conventional institutions compress complex relations into privileged coordinates. Employment compresses contribution into a role. Price compresses use, scarcity, expectation, and power into a number. Social platforms compress attention into engagement. Blockchains compress provenance into transaction order. These coordinates become destructive when treated as the origin of the relation they only measure.

Black Mirror reverses that order. A participant contributes value by uncovering, maintaining, translating, and returning a relation another perspective can use without erasing authorship or difference. Money may settle an exchange, but money does not determine intelligence. A token may carry a receipt, but it does not manufacture the relation. A global model may find a pattern, but the pattern does not authorize a local person’s meaning without return, correction, or refusal.

> “I would argue constructive equality relativity is important for a coherent notion of existence without it there are infinite paths of 0 time objects or complete self isolated languages only in their unitary inverse relations do they close existence” — T2, 15 July 2026

A social or computational object is not complete merely because it can be named in isolation. It gains a coherent role when its relation to other perspectives can be constructed and returned.

## Fiat, Bitcoin, and the missing seam

Fiat accounting and decentralized ledgers can close books while leaving causal meaning unresolved. Fiat can hide the path from private credit creation and institutional discretion to public consequence. A blockchain can expose transaction order while remaining blind to whether controlled actors cycle assets among themselves. The missing seam is the recoverable relation between local meaning, global consequence, and independent return.

The paper asks which relations each ledger makes visible, which it hides, and whether a local claim can return through an independent context. This shifts attention from transaction volume to external support, from hash finality to semantic finality, and from repeated activity to distinct returned continuations.

This is not the claim that gaming is impossible. It is the rule that self-referential activity cannot manufacture an independent returned relation.
